# Exhaust the Design Space

<!-- canonical-exception: cursor/plugins #571 tracks the upstream section cut that removed the antecedent sentence, leaving the product-design clause orphaned. -->
When a novel interaction or architectural decision has no established precedent, build 2-3 competing prototypes or sketches, compare them side by side, and only then commit. It also applies to product design decisions where user experience depends on feel, not logic.

# Frozen rubric — harness-neutral model/role policy seam

Score each candidate 1-5 per criterion; the rubric is frozen before any candidate spawns.

C1 Upstream fidelity (30%): preserves canonical cursor/plugins/pstack intent and one pinned workflow source; no second authority for role IDs.
C2 Maintenance burden (20%): incremental on existing schema/profile/receipt architecture; no second control plane or workflow runtime.
C3 Host-native correctness (25%): per-host catalogs resolved at runtime; no invented model availability; host-owned state untouched.
C4 Verifiability (15%): deterministic negative fixtures, fail-closed paths, receipt-bound evidence; static vs runtime proof distinguished.
C5 Boundary safety (10%): no policy fork import; qstack stays post-router overlay; no hidden durable store.

Selection rule: highest weighted total; ties prefer the smaller surface area.
